## Description
Credit cards feel like free money… so how do card companies make billions? Finn (14, piggy bank currently occupied by a moth) follows the money – with help from Mr. Swipe, a very shady talking credit card, and Grandma Ruth, the proudest "deadbeat" in America.

You'll learn: swipe fees, the grace period, how 22% interest snowballs, the minimum-payment trap, who really pays for rewards, the fees nobody mentions, why cards make you spend more – and 5 simple rules to make card companies earn $0 off you.
